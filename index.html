05 // DIVISION 01 · DEV SOLAIRE ENGINEERING

We remain the leader in solar energy in the DRC.

Dev Solaire specialises in solar items such as inverters, batteries, solar panels, regulators, and solar-powered appliances, alongside semi-wholesale, wholesale, and government project supply—backed by 19+ years in the DRC and a 12-month warranty on materials and installations.

01

Solar Electrical Installation

Professional installation of solar systems for homes, businesses, industries, and commercial projects.

02

Domestic & Industrial Solutions

Customised solar solutions for residential, commercial, agricultural, and industrial applications.

03

Solar Power Plant: Grid & Off-Grid

Design and installation of grid-tied, hybrid, and off-grid plants with capacity up to over 100 MWp.

04

Portable Kits & Solar Pumping

High-quality portable kits for boats, campers, and homes, plus solar water pumping for drinking water, livestock, and irrigation.

05

Street & Public Lighting

Energy-efficient solar street lighting with built-in batteries lasting up to 72 hours and a lifespan of over 5 years.

06

Maintenance & After-Sales

Routine maintenance, diagnostics, repairs, and a 12-month warranty on all materials and installations.
